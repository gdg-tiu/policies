# GDG on Campus TIU — Policies

This repository contains the official policies, guidelines, and community standards governing the GDG on Campus TIU community.

These policies are intended to establish clear expectations for participation, communication, events, and representation of the community, while maintaining a respectful, inclusive, safe, and professional environment for all members.

## Policies

### Community

- **[Code of Conduct](./code-of-conduct.md)** — Standards of behavior and expectations for all members participating in GDG on Campus TIU community spaces, activities, and events.

### Events

Additional event-specific policies and guidelines may be published as required.

## Scope

These policies apply to GDG on Campus TIU community spaces, activities, events, and official representations, whether online or offline.

Individual events may introduce additional rules or requirements. Such rules supplement, but do not replace, the applicable community policies.

## Updates

Policies may be reviewed and updated by the GDG on Campus TIU Organizing Team when necessary.

The latest version published in this repository represents the current version of the applicable policy unless otherwise stated.

## Reporting

If you believe a policy has been violated or have a concern regarding community conduct, please refer to the reporting procedure described in the applicable policy.
